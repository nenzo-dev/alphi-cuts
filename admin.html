Hours & slots

Clients get a chime and notification this many minutes before their slot, and the page rings when the slot starts. Use 0 for no heads-up.

Cancelled bookings are deleted straight away (unless they were paid online), and missed ones the day after. Style request photos are removed when you open this panel.
