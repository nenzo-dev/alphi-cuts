Get the app

Get an alert on your phone when it's your turn.

Android

The Android app rings when your slot starts, even when the app is closed.

Download for Android

  1. Tap Download, then open the file when it finishes.
  2. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap Install.
  3. Open AlPhi Cuts, book, and tap Turn on alerts.

The Android app is on its way. Until then, open this site in Chrome and choose "Add to Home screen" from the menu.

iPhone

Open this site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

On iPhone, alerts ring only while the site is open. On the day of your booking, keep it open in Safari (it can stay in the background), and add the booking to your calendar as a backup.
